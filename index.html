Master the basics in 3 minutes

1. When to do CPR?

When someone is unconscious and NOT breathing (or only gasping). Every minute without CPR reduces survival by 10%.

2. Call Emergency Services

If you're alone, call FIRST. If there are others, shout "You, call emergency and bring an AED!"

3. Hand Position

Heel of one hand in the CENTER of the chest. Other hand on top, fingers interlaced. Arms straight.

💡 What about mouth-to-mouth?

If you don't want to, DON'T DO IT. Hands-only CPR is very effective for adults. Act!
